Captain Bear 2: Support

How do I play?

Use the arrow buttons to walk, or tap a tile and Bear walks there. Bear can't jump, so look for stairs, ladders, crates, mushrooms and switches. Spin the world with the round arrows to find hidden paths. GRAB picks up and throws pinecones at bees.

I'm stuck

Every world has been checked by a computer solver and can be finished with every treat. Try Undo, spin the camera to look behind walls, and remember that crumbling tiles only work once and keys open only one gate each.

No sound?

Check that Music and Sound effects are on in the Menu, and that your device volume is up.
