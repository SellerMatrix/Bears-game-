## Description (4000)
Marbella Run is an arcade road trip with rocket boosters.

Start under the palms of Marbella's Golden Mile and race the clock 41 km along the Costa del Sol, past the Rock of Gibraltar to windy Tarifa, over the bridge into Portugal, round the cliffs of the Algarve to Lisbon, and home again through Seville and Ronda. The sun sets as you go: race through golden hour, a starry night on the Algarve and sunrise on the road home.

ROCKETS, LOOPS AND CORKSCREWS
Your car runs on rockets. Boost past 260 km/h, hop over traffic with the underside thrusters, and hit 270 km/h to stay on the road upside down. Too slow and you fall off.

CHOOSE YOUR ROUTE
Keep right after Puerto Banús for the AP-7 toll motorway: no traffic and a much higher top speed. Keep left at Sotogrande for the marina road, and look out for the slipway...

THREE ROCKET CARS
- Rocket Roadster: a wedge supercar with a glass canopy. The all-rounder.
- Sol Spider: an 80s open-top cruiser. Best grip, biggest rocket tank.
- Aero-X: a long-tail hypercar. The fastest, but it slides more and burns rockets quickly.

MADE FOR PLAYING ANYWHERE
- 8 stages, checkpoints, medals and records for every car
- Three original synthwave radio stations
- Touch controls with auto-accelerate, keyboard and game controller support
- Graphics settings from LOW to HIGH
- Works fully offline
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking
