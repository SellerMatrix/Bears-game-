## Description
Bear can't jump. But he can think.

Winter is on its way, and Bear needs a full tummy before he curls up in his cozy den. Travel from spring blossoms to frozen fjords across 12 hand-crafted diorama worlds, gathering honey, salmon and berries.

Every world is a little 3D puzzle box. Spin it around to spot hidden paths, then find the clever route to the golden honeycomb.

• Slide across slippery ice (and watch the crates slide too)
• Bounce off giant mushrooms over walls and rivers
• Push crates into rivers to build bridges
• Flip red and blue switch blocks
• Unlock gates with honey keys, but each key works only once
• Cross petals, leaves and lily pads that crumble behind you
• Push crates onto golden plates to raise shining bridges
• Pop through hollow tree stumps
• Throw pinecones at grumpy bees

FOUR SEASONS, FOUR LOOKS
Cherry blossom meadows, sunny salmon rivers, a moonlit autumn forest lit only by Bear's headlamp, and snowy peaks, each with its own music and weather.

FIND EVERYTHING
Every world hides a secret golden acorn and a step challenge for puzzle pros.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
No ads. No in-app purchases. No timers. No internet needed. Unlimited undo. Suitable for all ages.
