HOW TO PLAY

DRIVEReach each checkpoint before the clock hits zero. Every checkpoint adds time. 8 stages, 41 km, Marbella back to Marbella.
ROCKETSBoost past 260 km/h. The tank refills slowly, and fully at every checkpoint.
LOOPSYou need 270 km/h to stay on the road upside down. Fire the rockets before the loop or you fall off.
HOPThe underside thrusters jump you over traffic. Costs a little rocket fuel.
AP-7After Puerto Banús keep RIGHT for the toll motorway: no traffic, much higher top speed.
MARINAAt Sotogrande keep LEFT for the marina road. Look for the slipway...
KEYBOARDUP/W gas · DOWN/S brake · LEFT/RIGHT steer · SPACE rockets · SHIFT hop · P pause · M mute
TOUCHLeft side: steer. Right side: rockets, hop, brake (gas is automatic, can be changed in Settings)
GAMEPADStick/D-pad steer · A/RT gas · X/LT brake · B/RB rockets · Y/LB hop · START pause
