Marbella Run privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Marbella Run is a racing game. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (graphics, volume, speed units, camera, controls).
  • Your records (best times and scores per car) and which car you last chose.

These are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. They are deleted when you delete the app.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No network requests while you play: everything the game needs is inside the app.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support link on the game's App Store page.
