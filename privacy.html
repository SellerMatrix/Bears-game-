Captain Bear 2: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

The short version

Captain Bear 2 does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

What the game stores

Your game progress (which worlds you have cleared, treats and golden acorns found, challenge badges) and your settings (music, sound, camera and graphics options) are saved only on your own device. They never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

Internet use

The game works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
